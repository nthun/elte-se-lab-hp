I’m Joanelli Kamilla, and I have always been drawn to the hidden processes behind seemingly simple human reactions why the same situation can feel completely different to two people, and how stress and emotions shape what we think and do. After completing my BA in Psychology in Pécs, I joined the Stress and Emotions research group in 2026 to explore these questions from a scientific perspective. Through my work in the lab, I hope to deepen my understanding of psychological research and gain practical experience in uncovering the mechanisms behind human behaviour.
